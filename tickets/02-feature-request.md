# Ticket 02: Make the approval queue easier to work through

**From:** Deal Desk Managers (EMEA and NA)
**Priority:** Medium

Right now the approval queue is one long list. On a busy day we have 40+ deals
waiting and we waste time scrolling.

What we would like:

1. Filter the queue by region and by team, so each manager sees their own deals.
2. Sort the biggest deals first, so we handle the most money first.
3. A summary at the top: how many deals are waiting, and how much money is
   waiting on us.

We are not picky about the exact look. We just want to clear the queue faster
and be able to tell leadership how much is stuck on approvals.

Thanks,
The Deal Desk managers
